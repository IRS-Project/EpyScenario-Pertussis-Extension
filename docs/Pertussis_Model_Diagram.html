Pertussis transmission model — SEIRS with partial-immunity track

8 compartments, 7 age bands (0-1, 1-6, 7-10, 11-19, 20-49, 50-64, 65+). Each transition is labelled with its symbol and (default value). Structure adapted from Wearing & Rohani (2009).

Initial conditions (defaults): infected 0.1% · immune 90% · partial-infection share 33% · partial-immune (Sₚ) share 30%.    Force of infection on S is λ = β·(I + σ·Iₚ); on Sₚ it is δ·λ. β is calibrated so the dominant next-generation eigenvalue equals R₀.

Conventions: solid arrows = flows of individuals (labelled with their per-capita rate); dashed arrows = the force of infection (an influence that depends on I and Iₚ, not a flow). Closed population — no births or deaths: susceptibility is replenished by waning (ω₁/ω₂/ω₃), not by births, a simplification of the Wearing–Rohani framework (the engine represents compartment-to-compartment transitions only). μ and μₚ denote recovery rates here, not mortality.
